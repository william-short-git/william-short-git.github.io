🌟 How to Play:

• Listen & Pick: Click the speaker button to hear the target word, then tap the matching card!

• XP & Relics: Gain XP on correct hits and defeats to level up and draft damage relics!

• Boss Chests: Defeating bosses drops a Treasure Chest with 10-50 coins, health potions, and relics!

• 10 Levels per Set: Difficulty increases every 5 waves (CVC → Blends → Vowel Teams → Multi-syllable)!

• 10% Heal: 3 correct answers in a row restores 10% of your HP!
